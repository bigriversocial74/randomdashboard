# Section 11 Acceptance Criteria

- Two to five visible suppliers can be selected.
- Weights normalize to 100%.
- Recommendation calculations are transparent.
- Supplier, scorecard, PO, contract, inventory, terms, risk, and company-coverage evidence is shown.
- Missing evidence is explicit.
- Company scope and permissions are enforced server-side.
- Saved decisions work in Demo Data and Production Data after migration.
- Preferred and alternate suppliers are stored with rationale.
- Approval routing creates a review record, reviewer notification, and audit event.
- CSV export protects spreadsheet consumers from formula injection.
- MySQL 8.0 and MariaDB 10.11 both accept the deferred migration.
- Cumulative Sections 1–11 quality gates pass before merge.
