**Headline**
One intelligent operating layer for purchasing, inventory, suppliers, projects and savings.

**Support copy**
Gruber AI connects five distinct businesses through shared data, centralized leverage, operational workflows and human-supervised AI intelligence.

## 2. The Current Challenge

**Headline**
Five businesses. Different workflows. Shared opportunities.

**Story points**
- Supplier demand is fragmented.
- Inventory is distributed across companies and locations.
- The same item may be purchased at different prices.
- Late orders and stockouts are difficult to prioritize.
- Leadership does not have one live financial and operational view.

**Animation**
Separate business cards drift apart, then connect through a shared data line.
